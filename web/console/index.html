Data boundary, evaluation and limits

Manually authored synthetic text represents notes, intake and fax content. No OCR, EMR or EDI ingestion is implemented. No model or external vendor receives data. Browser requests stay on loopback. Records and audit receipts live in server memory until Reset or restart. Receipts omit raw content, but this console shows synthetic attempts.

One shared local session, no user isolation or reviewer authentication. Do not enter patient data. Source labels and dates are caller assertions, not authenticated evidence. A held write uses a separate dry-run runtime. Approval creates a new screened request, without a durable review queue or atomic audit transaction.

Six curated attempts demonstrate behavior, not accuracy. The repeatable evaluation reports baseline comparisons, false blocks and missed encoded instructions and false clinical claims. Workflow time, rework, adoption and patient outcomes have not been measured.

Reusable records and memory services share the deterministic gate. Rules are the baseline, no model is needed to demonstrate this boundary. English synthetic strings only. This does not establish clinical safety across documents, populations or languages.
